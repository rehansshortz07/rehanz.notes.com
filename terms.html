Guidelines for accessing, uploading, and using academic resources.

By accessing or using Learn-Engineering, users agree to use the platform responsibly and primarily for legitimate educational and academic purposes.

1. Academic Use

Learn-Engineering is intended to support engineering students and faculty by providing access to academic resources organized according to branch and semester.

2. Uploaded Materials

Users who are permitted to upload resources should only upload materials that are relevant to the academic curriculum and that they are authorized to share.

3. Copyright & Ownership

Users must respect the copyright and intellectual-property rights of authors, publishers, faculty members, and other content owners. Users should not upload or distribute copyrighted material without appropriate permission or authorization.

4. Prohibited Content

The platform must not be used to upload malicious files, harmful content, illegal material, spam, or content unrelated to legitimate academic use.

5. Accuracy of Resources

Academic resources may be uploaded by authorized users. Users should exercise appropriate judgment and, where necessary, confirm important academic information with their faculty or official institutional sources.

6. Responsible Use

Users must not attempt to interfere with the operation, security, storage systems, database, authentication mechanisms, or other technical components of the platform.

7. Removal of Content

Resources may be removed when they are found to be inappropriate, unauthorized, inaccurate, duplicated, outdated, or in violation of these guidelines.

8. Institutional Adoption

Learn-Engineering is a student-developed initiative. If the platform is later adopted or maintained by an educational institution, its policies and administrative requirements may supersede these initial guidelines.
