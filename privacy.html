Privacy Policy

How Learn-Engineering handles account information and academic resources.

Learn-Engineering respects the privacy of students, faculty members, and other authorized users. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected and how that information is used to operate the academic resource platform.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on the features used, the platform may collect information such as a user's name, email address, university or institutional identification number, branch, semester, account information, and other details required for the platform's academic functionality.

2. How Information Is Used

Information is used to provide authentication, maintain user profiles, organize academic resources, manage permissions, and provide the platform's educational services.

3. Academic Resources

Files and academic materials uploaded to the platform may contain educational information intended for students and faculty. Uploaded resources are stored and delivered through the platform's configured storage infrastructure.

4. Data Security

Learn-Engineering uses authentication, database access controls, server-side services, and configured security policies to help protect user information and academic resources from unauthorized access.

5. Third-Party Services

The platform may use third-party infrastructure and services to provide authentication, database, application hosting, and file storage functionality. Such services may process information as necessary to provide their respective infrastructure services.

6. Personal Information

Learn-Engineering is not intended to collect unnecessary personal information. Users should avoid uploading sensitive personal information that is unrelated to legitimate academic activities.

7. Sharing of Information

Personal information is not intentionally sold or used for unrelated commercial purposes. Information may, however, be processed by the infrastructure providers used to operate the platform or where required for legitimate security, legal, or institutional purposes.

8. User Responsibility

Users are responsible for keeping their account credentials secure and for providing accurate information when creating or maintaining an account.

9. Policy Updates

This Privacy Policy may be updated as Learn-Engineering develops, introduces new features, or is adopted and administered by an educational institution.
