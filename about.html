About TechVault-IN

A centralized digital platform for organizing and accessing engineering academic resources.

TechVault-IN is a student-developed academic resource platform created to provide a centralized, structured, and accessible way to manage and access engineering study materials.

The platform organizes academic resources according to engineering branch and semester, making it easier for students to find materials relevant to their current academic requirements.

TechVault-IN is designed to support the distribution and accessibility of resources such as:

  • Lecture Notes
  • Question Papers
  • Laboratory Manuals
  • Assignments
  • Syllabus and Reference Materials
  • Other Academic Study Materials

Our Purpose

To reduce the difficulty students face when searching for academic materials by providing a structured digital platform where resources can be organized and accessed according to branch and semester.
