About Learn-Engineering

Created to make academic resources easier to access and organize.

Learn-Engineering is a student-developed academic resource platform created to provide a centralized and organized way to access study materials.

The platform organizes academic resources according to engineering branch and semester, making it easier for students to find the materials relevant to their current academics.

The platform is intended to support the distribution and accessibility of resources such as:

  • Lecture Notes
  • Question Papers
  • Laboratory Manuals
  • Assignments
  • Other Academic Study Materials

Our Purpose

To reduce the difficulty students face when searching for academic materials by providing a structured digital platform where resources can be organized and accessed according to branch and semester.
